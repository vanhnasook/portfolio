About

I edit film & design for Lao brands.

I'm Vanhnasook Chanthamith — a video editor and graphic designer based in Vientiane, Laos. For over a decade I've been cutting broadcast video at the national federation and designing brand identities, packaging, and social campaigns for local businesses and tech startups.

My work lives where motion meets layout: short promos, app launch films, print collateral, and product packaging that needs to look the same on a phone screen and a printed tin. I currently design at Wayha Sokxay Technology and take on independent creative work on the side.

01

Cut for the platform

A 30-second TV segment, an Instagram reel, and a 60-second app promo all breathe differently. I cut for where it'll actually be watched.

02

Design that prints

Every poster, flyer, and label I ship is built to survive the printer — real bleed, CMYK-safe color, and proper margins.

03

AI as a tool, not a style

I use AI daily for ideation, batching, and asset generation — but the final call is always taste. The tool is fast; the eye is mine.
